Melnitz Privacy Policy

Melnitz accesses only the Google account data of its single operator, Jonathan Leek, under OAuth scopes he grants (Gmail read/modify, Calendar read-only).

Data is processed on Jonathan's own hardware, is not shared with any third party, and is not used for any purpose other than assisting Jonathan.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
